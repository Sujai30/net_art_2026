Midnight Thoughts

At 2 a.m. the city feels different. The streets are quieter, the lights glow softer, and every thought somehow becomes louder. Sometimes I sit by my window and wonder how many other people are awake thinking about their lives.


Things I Think About

  • unfinished conversations
  • future plans
  • songs that never get old
  • places I still want to visit

One song I always replay is found on Spotify. Music somehow makes loneliness feel poetic instead of painful.

Small Reminders

  1. Keep moving forward
  2. Don't compare your journey to others
  3. Take breaks when needed

Some memories stay beautiful even after they end. Some people leave quietly but still remain part of your story forever.


Late Night Note

The world moves fast during the day, but at night it finally feels possible to breathe.
