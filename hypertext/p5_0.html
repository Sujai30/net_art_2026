Three Pills

Fragmentation


A pressure blooms in your chest that shouldn’t exist.

Not pain—more like a memory of pain replayed in the wrong body.


Images flicker:

  • hands
  • impact
  • your name—shouted
  • the floor arriving too fast

You blink and the street returns.

You hold still, afraid the moment will leave before you understand it.

“DO NOT TAKE ALL AT ONCE.”
