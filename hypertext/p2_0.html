Three Pills

The Street


The city moves with purpose. You move with it.

No one bumps you. No one makes space.

At a crosswalk, people flow around the place you stand.

It’s as if the world has learned a new rule: do not acknowledge.


Your wrist aches.

You pull your sleeve back and find a bruise—yellowed, old, almost gone.

You don’t remember earning it.


Your fingers touch the container in your pocket.

You read the label again.

“TAKE ONE ONLY WHEN NECESSARY.”


Pills remaining: 0

You wish you had more memory pills.

Keep walking
