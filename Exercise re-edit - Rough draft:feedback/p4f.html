After The Turning

The stairs disappear behind me as I choose not to descend. I hear the faint rustle of paper in the darkness below—pages being turned by invisible hands. I tell myself that secrets can remain secrets, at least for a little longer.

Light from a crack in the ceiling reveals the dust in the air. The corridor ahead straightens. My breath fogs less; the temperature has risen a fraction. Somewhere beyond this turning, another choice awaits.
