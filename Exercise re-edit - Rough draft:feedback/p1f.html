After The Ringing

The ringing stops on its own. The sudden silence is not relief but something heavier. I keep the receiver untouched, the weight of the pill bottle unchanged in my fist. The corridor seems to shrink, as if my refusal closes off some possibility.

I walk forward without the comfort or burden of any memory. The walls breathe quietly, the floor creaks under my shoes. In the distance a faint light shimmers, promising another choice.
