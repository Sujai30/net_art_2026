After The Letter

I step over the sealed envelope without opening it. The wax seal cracks under my shoe and leaves a faint red smear on the stone. I tell myself that not knowing is a kind of mercy, but I still feel the letter's weight in the air.

As I walk on, the smell of salt and sun bleeds into the corridor. Somewhere beyond these walls, waves are breaking and birds cry. I let the sounds seep into me without trying to claim them. Acceptance can come without understanding.
