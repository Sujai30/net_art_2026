After The Ledger

I let the desk full of papers and numbers fade behind me. I do not turn the pages or follow the lines of ink to their conclusions. My pockets remain heavier with pills and lighter with regret.

A smell of frying oil and salt drifts from a doorway up ahead. I recognise it but do not pursue it. My stomach growls; my heart aches; my feet continue to move. Choices withheld are choices nonetheless.
