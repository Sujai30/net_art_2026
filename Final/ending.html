Crossing Over

I turn the handle and step through. There is no bright light, no tunnel, no choir. There is instead a quiet landscape that feels like everything I have ever touched.

The memories I chose to keep shape what I see. If I took none, I bring nothing and the landscape is blank. If I took some, the colours and sounds reflect them. If I took all, every blade of grass hums with resonance. Either way, the unknown is no longer frightening. It is simply what comes next.

Understanding, it turns out, is less about facts and more about acceptance. I cannot change my death. I cannot change my choices. But I can decide how to carry them across this threshold. I breathe in. I let go.
