The Observer

You were not supposed to find this.

The corridor was designed to move you forward — one pill, one memory, one step toward the door. But you stopped. You counted. You pressed the same place three times as though you suspected something was there.

You were right.

I have been here since the beginning, watching you choose. Every pill you took or left behind, every door you opened or passed — I noted it. Not to judge. Only to remember, because someone must.

We are the same, you and the one whose life this is. You are both reviewers now. You both stand slightly outside a life, deciding what it meant.

Go back. The corridor still ends the same way. But you will carry this moment with you through it.
